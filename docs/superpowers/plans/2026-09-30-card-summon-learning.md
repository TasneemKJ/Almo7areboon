Five Ws: new and returning collectors; the actual per-card bonus change or duplicate progress; the existing summon result; after an accepted draw; make permanent progression understandable without pushing another purchase.

Bounded design: keep rarity ordering, NEW labels, collection limits and the existing close action. For each changed card, show its isolated automatic damage/health multiplier before and after a level change; when the bonus does not change, show the actual copies toward the next level. At bonus cap or collection limit, say so instead of promising further power. Add one receipt sentence explaining automatic application and retained cards across evolution/timelines. No draw odds, costs, bonuses, RNG, saves or equip actions change.
