# Battle banner and Journey upgrade

The user authorizes autonomous improvements to visuals, retention, game loop and gameplay, retaining the mobile-first goal. This is an architectural enhancement of the existing Phaser/DOM game. Design and execution decisions are delegated by the user's explicit “without my interventions”; do not introduce approval checkpoints.

## Direction
Preserve the illustrated Levantine settlements, armies, enamel navy and brass, paper dialogs, and original campaign. Strengthen the play loop: scout a wave, deploy a mixed company, earn momentum, choose an order, collect existing rewards, choose a next objective. Sessions remain short, pauseable and playable offline. No backend, purchases, timers demanding a return, new random reward economy, or unmeasured retention claims.

The signature is a company battle banner. It appears as a compact stitched strip in the deployment surface, with a momentum gauge and two native buttons. The existing storybook opens into tactical depth; a Journey modal connects existing seals, milestones, routes and collection with direct navigation. Larger visual emphasis belongs to actionable readiness and state changes, not extra permanent panels over combat.

## Orders
A battle begins with 0 momentum. Successful player deployments earn 12; real enemy kills earn 8, capped at 100. Orders cost 60, last 10 simulation seconds and cannot overlap. Advance gives normal player troop attacks +20% damage and movement +15%. Hold reduces incoming troop and gate damage by 25%. Skills gain no offensive multiplier. No healing, refunds, reward multipliers or new enemy scaling. Manual pause, menus and hidden pages freeze duration. Unavailable, invalid, paused and duplicate casts reject before mutation. Retry/reload clear transient charge and orders. The existing save schema remains unchanged; optional additive ordersCast statistics default to zero in old receipts. Orders are available in original and Chronicle battles, including expeditions.

## Journey and rewards
Build recommendations from authoritative profile state: unclaimed earned quests first, missing current-chapter seals, affordable troop/production unlocks, then story routes and collection. Reuse existing daily and milestone rewards with their once-only claims. Show three parallel milestone ladders (wins, kills, deployments), each with its nearest unclaimed milestone, measured progress and honest reward. Do not invent grants or add mandatory daily mechanics. Ready and results provide Journey entry; the combat surface exposes it through the existing quest menu. Navigation preserves pending results and manual pause. Reuse modal isolation, close row and guarded action boundaries.

## Presentation
Use canonical CSS tokens (--ink, --paper, --navy, --gold, --blue) and serif chapter headings with Trebuchet utility text. Orders have icon, label, countdown and semantic disabled state, so color is supplemental. A thin canvas aura/ring under living player troops and a company pennant identify the active order; reduced motion keeps static marks and countdown. Gauge changes use transforms, no per-frame DOM construction. Unit portraits retain legibility, readiness has a brass edge and clear food shortfall label. No art regeneration is needed; extend shipped assets and original vector marks. Preserve 44px controls, safe areas, scrolling dialog close coverage and width at 320px; a short phone may legitimately scroll the shell. No forced orientation, external fonts or additional dependencies.

## Verification
Source regressions must fail before implementation: charge only on accepted actions/kills, no overflow or double cast, pause freezes order expiry, offensive and defensive combat math, reset and old-save restoration. Journey tests cover new and veteran profiles, capped completed ladders, receipt preservation and claim navigation. Real Chromium/WebKit browser QA covers narrow/tall phones, native order taps, paused rejection, Journey reward claims and routes, rotation, reduced motion, offline first return, save/reload and 20 cycles. Inspect representative canvas and UI screenshots. Final fresh independent whole-branch review follows implementation. Reliability merge #152 remains separate and must complete first.
