# Folktale tactics expansion

Approved scope: implement the twenty ideas from the conversation as an integrated first release, then raise a PR. Preserve the existing game, storybook art and save ownership. Do not merge.

## Intent / 5 Ws
Who: portrait-phone players. What: a small-army folktale tactics expansion. Where: the existing TypeScript/Phaser game. When: tactical decisions during combat, preparation between encounters. Why: legible victories, memorable people and meaningfully different replays.

## IDEAL
Identify the shallow deployment choices and disconnected progression. Define success as accessible gameplay paths for all twenty ideas without twenty menus. Explore a contextual Rally order and an illustrated journey. Act through deterministic simulation and existing guarded UI actions. Look back through unit tests, migrations, production browser checks and explicit limitations.

## Scope
1. Gather up to six newly deployed troops, then release together.
2. Living forward defenders protect ranged allies; heavy strikes break protection briefly.
3. Freeze-to-heavy shatter and Meteor-to-cover interactions.
4. Occupancy-controlled cover, supply and lantern landmarks.
5. Siege, escort, last watch, rescue and night objective rules.
6. A real Bell Keeper actor with a telegraphed, interruptible summon.
7. Three deterministic commander personalities.
8. Two captains replace the Food Drop slot; no additional skill row.
9. Two recurring named veterans with saved victories and insignia.
10. Three mutually exclusive earned behavioral tales; old cards retain value.
11. Illustrated route forks for unlocked chapters.
12. Oven, courtyard and workshop restoration; no production timers.
13. The first cart/scout rescue records a later chapter consequence.
14. Optional night-route lantern control, with readable shadow troops.
15. Three safe post-battle discoveries form one connected optional tale.
16. Shared ink/gouache palette, existing painted assets and ground-plane depth sorting.
17. Readable order, protection, shatter and bell feedback, respecting reduced motion.
18. Contextual first-encounter teaching, not a mandatory dashboard tutorial.
19. A three-stage expedition with bounded supplies and saved inter-battle checkpoints.
20. Authored unlit, overgrown and reluctant-ally timeline variations.

## Safety and acceptance
Schema 5 migrates 1–4 and protects unknown future versions. Chronicle data is bounded and validated. Existing save-session ownership, backup/import, pending-victory and pause rules remain authoritative. Terminal outcomes settle once. New controls use the existing action guard. No new dependency, backend, account, purchase or scheduler.

Every idea needs actual integration, not just a definition. A green unit suite alone is not physical-mobile/Safari or artistic acceptance. The PR must distinguish shipped scope from unverified performance, device and polish limits.
