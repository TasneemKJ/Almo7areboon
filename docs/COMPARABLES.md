# «المحاربون» · Comparables (learn the principle, never copy)

These are already studied in DESIGN.md, docs/COMPETITIVE.md and tas-studio-notes/COMPARABLES-almo7areboon.md: Clash Royale, Kingdom Rush, Kingdom Two Crowns, Darkest Dungeon, Slay the Spire, Rush Royale, Plants vs. Zombies, Into the Breach, Bad North, XCOM, Age of War and Frostpunk. This file covers five others.

## 2026-11-16: five popular defence and lane games

### Thronefall (Grizzly Games, 2023)
- **Loop:** build by day, then hold one night of waves. A ruler on horseback fights in person.
- **Art:** low-poly minimalism, praised as stylish. One review says the minimal visuals blur the action.
- **Story and onboarding:** no story. The first night teaches everything.
- **Players:** OpenCritic about 8.6, called "streamlined, intuitive, and thrilling". Complaints: thin content, difficulty spikes that kill choice, and troop management that feels unpolished.
- **Earns:** premium on PC and Switch. A user review gives about $13 (unverified).

### Bloons TD 6 (Ninja Kiwi, 2018)
- **Loop:** place and upgrade towers along a track, with deep upgrade paths and heroes.
- **Art:** bright cartoon, readable at a glance.
- **Story and onboarding:** none needed. The first map is the tutorial.
- **Players:** loved by millions, 4.5+ on the stores, called "one of the few premium mobile games that feels worth it". The main complaint is in-app purchases inside a paid game: Monkey Money continues cost $2 to $55, and players say the grind pushes them to pay.
- **Earns:** $4.99 to buy, plus in-app purchases.

### The Battle Cats (PONOS, 2014)
- **Loop:** a side-scrolling lane: spend a refilling wallet to send cats and push to the enemy base.
- **Art:** deliberately crude, absurd cats (a cat with legs, a cat in a tank) whose silliness is the charm.
- **Story and onboarding:** a joke world map of invasion, with humour in every unit's description.
- **Players:** they love that it's free with no forced ads, with optional ad rewards. They complain about gacha drop rates, energy waits and Cat Food prices (about $99 for the biggest pack, per a user report).
- **Earns:** free-to-play gacha, Cat Food currency and optional ads.

### Stick War: Legacy (Max Games Studios, 2015)
- **Loop:** mine gold, train units, push the line, and take direct control of any single unit.
- **Art:** stick figures with huge readability and almost no detail.
- **Story and onboarding:** a campaign of nations.
- **Players:** 4.8 from about 77k App Store reviews, called addictive and challenging. About 5% of reviews complain about ads and monetization.
- **Earns:** free with ads (an estimated 57% of revenue) and gems from $2.99 to $27.99, plus "Disable Ads" at $2.99. Loot boxes are disclosed.

### Arknights (Hypergryph, 2019) (not searched this round, from memory: unverified)
- **Loop:** place named operators on a grid against waves.
- **Art:** moody, painterly character art and a sombre world.
- **Story and onboarding:** a long, dark story with characters players care about.
- **Players:** praise the art, music and characters; complain that the gacha is expensive.
- **Earns:** free-to-play character gacha plus skins.

## What we take (principles, made our own)
1. **Never sell the way out of a loss** (Bloons). The strongest backlash against a loved paid game is the continue for sale and a grind tuned to push it. Almo7areboon keeps losses as story (named soldiers, scars) and never sells a revive or a skipped wave. What we sell stays content and comfort already in the game.
2. **A roster is loved for its character, not its stats** (Battle Cats, Arknights). Absurd humour or named, drawn people make units into someone. This supports #92, the named soldiers: one dry fusha line each and a face we remember, so a loss costs something.
3. **One clear rhythm, and no spike that removes choice** (Thronefall). Day to build, night to hold, a minimal HUD in the chaos. Tune the waves with the bot simulation so no wave has only one answer.

No backlog item this round: #92 (named soldiers) is already next to build, and lesson 2 strengthens it.
