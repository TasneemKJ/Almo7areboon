## Binding design
- Existing navy/brass/paper theme and Georgia/Trebuchet typography; no new assets, fonts, effects or palette.
- Existing button markup, ARIA names, values, keyboard handlers and Game.dispatch stay byte-identical.
- Active troop cards: 88px total with name, role, portrait, price and progress bands; no ellipsis or hiding of costs.
- Navigation: all four labeled destinations remain native controls at least 44px high; secondary panels align to that boundary.
- Landscape rail: 300–340px at 36vw; no core action moves into a disclosure.
- No simulation, save, balance, audio, timer or persistent state changes. Mute/reduced-motion ownership remains unchanged.
- Short-phone scroll fallback remains; never clip actions to force a prettier screenshot.
