# Village Courtyard Answer

## Observable gap

The current settlement is an active witness when danger arrives: windows empty,
lamps dim, the score darkens, and a sparse watch pulse sounds. When sustained
safety begins, the same system only fades back toward its quiet mix. The village
does not acknowledge that meaningful transition, so recovery reads as an audio
parameter returning to normal rather than people answering from home.

## IDEAL

- **Identity:** the fictional Levantine dusk settlement is a participant in the
  defence, not decorative scenery.
- **Destination:** after the existing safety hysteresis proves that pressure has
  passed, the player hears one gentle, chapter-coloured courtyard answer and can
  understand that the village is recovering.
- **Essence:** a restrained two-note reply, derived from the chapter's original
  score root, answers the alarm without sounding like a reward or a new command.
- **Approach:** record the authoritative alarm-to-recovery transition in the
  transient village mood owner; let the existing atmosphere player consume that
  serial exactly once through its current audibility, pause, visibility, output,
  and eight-voice ownership gates.
- **Limits:** no gameplay, save, economy, result, menu, preference, or timing
  changes; no replay after muted/hidden time; no cue on victory, defeat, reset,
  ordinary quiet, or merely falling alarm mix.

## Five Ws

- **Who:** a player defending any of the six settlements with Sound and
  Atmosphere enabled.
- **What:** one bounded two-note courtyard answer when `alarmed` authoritatively
  becomes `recovering`.
- **When:** only after the existing minimum alarm duration and six-second quiet
  hysteresis complete; never while paused, hidden, in a modal, or after a result.
- **Where:** the existing synthesized atmosphere bus and shared voice budget,
  spatially neutral so headphones are not required.
- **Why:** make the village feel alive and turn the already-existing recovery
  transition into readable, diegetic combat feedback without another UI surface.

## Acceptance

1. The mood owner increments one recovery serial only on each real
   `alarmed -> recovering` transition and resets it with battle identity.
2. The atmosphere player emits one answer for a fresh serial, derives its two
   finite frequencies from the current chapter score, and releases its one voice.
3. Disabled/paused/hidden/result audio consumes the serial without replaying it
   later; a context or chapter replacement cannot leak the old answer.
4. Existing alarm entry/pulse behavior, source tests, production build, voice
   budgets, preferences, combat state, and saves remain unchanged.
5. Automated browser evidence exposes the consumed recovery transition at phone
   widths without relying on audible subjective judgement.
