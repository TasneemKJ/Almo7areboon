# Village Watchfire Relay

## Observable gap

The authoritative incoming-wave preview already paints a small road omen during
the final four battle seconds. Current-build phone evidence shows that this omen
is truthful and unobtrusive, but the settlement behind it remains visually
unaware until enemies are close enough to trigger the separate village alarm.
The world therefore communicates the warning through one isolated battlefield
prop instead of letting the inhabited village pass the news toward the gate.

## IDEAL

- **Identity:** the fictional Levantine dusk settlement keeps watch with the
  player; its measured lamps are working parts of the defence, not decoration.
- **Destination:** when an actual scheduled wave enters the existing four-second
  warning window, the settlement passes a restrained watchfire relay toward the
  road omen, reinforcing that danger is imminent without another HUD surface.
- **Essence:** chapter-coloured lamp halos brighten in a short, shape-backed
  sequence; small upright ink-and-pigment beacon marks make the signal readable
  without relying on colour alone.
- **Approach:** derive a finite immutable watch signal from the same authoritative
  `WavePreview` already used by the road omen, then project it only through the
  village's measured lamp anchors and current HUD exclusions.
- **Limits:** no combat, spawn, wave, clock, save, reward, economy, input, menu,
  preference, audio, or progression changes; no independent presentation clock.

## Five Ws

- **Who:** any player defending one of the six painted settlements.
- **What:** a bounded village watchfire relay using existing measured lamps and
  shape-distinct beacon strokes.
- **When:** only during the authoritative final four seconds before a non-empty
  incoming wave; it disappears when that preview ends and freezes under pause.
- **Where:** inside HUD-clear lamp regions in the existing ambience plane, behind
  actors and controls.
- **Why:** make the settlement feel alive while strengthening an already truthful
  tactical warning without adding text, controls, or menu complexity.

## Acceptance

1. The relay is absent outside a running, non-empty, finite four-second wave
   preview and cannot fabricate progress from malformed values.
2. Normal motion activates a bounded number of lamps in deterministic sequence;
   reduced motion presents one stable complete alert instead of a chasing relay.
3. Every beacon is registered to a measured lamp, excluded when its expanded
   region overlaps visible HUD, finite, immutable, and readable by shape plus
   pigment.
4. Pause supplies the same authoritative preview and produces a byte-stable
   frame; no presentation clock advances the signal.
5. The existing road omen, village alarm/recovery/verdict, restoration lights,
   source tests, production build, saves, and gameplay behavior remain unchanged.
6. Native Chromium evidence captures the current-build relay at phone widths and
   reduced motion; screenshots are inspected before merge.
